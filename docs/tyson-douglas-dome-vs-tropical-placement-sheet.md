## What the Sheet Shows

The planetary sign placements are closely aligned in the two longitude columns for this instant. The large differences visible here are chiefly the result of the **different axis and house contracts**: the declared dome-model view uses its direct UTC-degree Ascendant and Equal Houses, while the conventional reference uses local Placidus cusps. The map coordinates also differ because one map projects RA/declination to a Gleason polar field and the other arranges ecliptic longitude on a wheel.
