# Tyson–Douglas God–Agent Standard / Inverse Synergy

## Contract

God View is the fixed background field. Agent View is the topocentric Tokyo Dome event field assigned to the Ancient-Horizon Equal-House chart. Synthesis cross-maps each planet’s God sector with its Agent family. Standard and inverse orientations are preserved as separate records.

## Comparison with Territorial / Cluster

The Ancient-Horizon Territorial/Cluster layer selects Side A in standard orientation and Side B in inverse-180 orientation. The God–Agent synthesis selects neither orientation: it is neutral/tied in both. The two methods must remain reported separately; no composite winner is manufactured from their disagreement.

This is a single historical stress test and is not evidence of predictive validity.
